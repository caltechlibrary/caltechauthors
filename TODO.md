- [ ] **Harden the record pages' MathJax** (found 2026-10-27 in the irdmtools `doi2rdm` render check; evidence and reasoning in kb observation 557 and `irdmtools/DR-0005`). The pages load MathJax 3.2.2 `tex-mml-chtml.js` (`THEME_MATHJAX_CDN` in `invenio.cfg`) with the upstream `invenio_theme` inline config `inlineMath: [['$','$'], ['\\(','\\)']]` and no `packages`. That bundle preloads `require`, `autoload`, `newcommand` and `configmacros`, and its autoload maps `\href`, `\class`, `\style` and `\cssId` to the html extension, so those commands work in any text between `\(` and `\)` or `$` and `$`, including a depositor's description. `$` is also an inline delimiter, so two dollar signs in a title or description (for example a CrossRef book review title with `$115 (hb), $39.50 (pb)`) turn the text between them into math. Nothing changed yet; not tested.
